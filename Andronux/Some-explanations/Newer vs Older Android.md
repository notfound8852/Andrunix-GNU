
On newer android. most devices use a super partition and there the boot.img file ends up being a lightweight middle man.. init does exist inside these boot images but it's one job is to mount the super partition and then hand control to init from within the partition. This makes modifications on newer android much more challenging and this approach/setup likely doesn't work unless you are able to find the init.rc from with in /system and modify it manually with root privileges and dm-0 verity is disabled (so that it actually updates the init.rc).. But the risk here is that if you mess the file up.. congratulations you just found a custom boot recovery project for your entire weekend.. 
And me personally I haven't messed with newer android devices with root or even at the init level. It's simply cause I don't have one. 💀
